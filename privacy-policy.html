Privacy Policy

GForceMeter

Last updated: 24 May 2026

1. Introduction

GForceMeter (“we”, “our”, or “the App”) is a mobile application designed to measure and visualize real-time acceleration (G-force) using built-in motion sensors on your device.

This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information and what data may be processed while you use the App.

2. Information We Collect

We do not directly collect, store, or request any personally identifiable information from users.

The App does not require:

  • Account creation
  • Login or registration
  • Name, email, phone number, or address input

3. Sensor and Device Data

The App uses built-in device sensors such as accelerometer and gyroscope (if available).

This data is processed locally on your device and is used only to calculate and display real-time G-force values. It is not stored or transmitted to any external server.

4. Third-Party Services

Google AdMob

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements.

AdMob may automatically collect certain device information, including:

  • Advertising ID
  • Device information (model, operating system version)
  • Approximate usage data for ad personalization

This data is processed according to Google’s Privacy Policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share personal information collected directly by the App.

Third-party services may process limited data as described above.

6. Data Retention

We do not store any personal data on servers. Therefore, no user data is retained by the App.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed toward children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Security

All sensor processing occurs locally on the device. The App does not maintain any user database.

Third-party services follow standard industry security practices.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be reflected by changing the “Last updated” date.
